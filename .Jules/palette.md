## 2024-05-24 - Accessibility on Mobile Menu Toggles\n**Learning:** Icon-only mobile menu toggles need dynamic ARIA labels (e.g., 'Open menu' vs 'Close menu') and explicit association with their container via `aria-controls` to be fully accessible to screen readers.\n**Action:** Always verify mobile navigation patterns have explicit `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, and dynamic `aria-label` attributes on their toggle buttons.
